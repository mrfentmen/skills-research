# Suspense Scene Treatment: Bag Switch

## Title: "Wrong Train"

### Logline
A meticulous train conductor discovers a passenger's bag has been swapped, triggering a tense race against time to prevent a disaster while questioning his own perception.

### Act 1: The Setup
- **Setting:** A vintage steam train crossing the countryside at sunset. The conductor, ELLIOT (50s, methodical, takes pride in his routine), makes his final rounds check.
- **Inciting Incident:** During a compartment check, Elliot notices Seat 12's carry-on bag looks slightly different — the luggage tag font doesn't match the railway's standard, and the zipper alignment is off.
- **Initial Conflict:** Elliot opens the bag to investigate. Inside: a wool coat, a leather-bound journal, and a dense manila envelope labeled in hasty ballpoint: *"URGENT — DO NOT DELAY."*

### Act 2: The Discovery
- **Complication:** Elliot alerts the train manager. They check the manifest; the bag assigned to Seat 12 is accounted for, but the physical bag in the overhead compartment is wrong.
- **Escalation:** A new passenger, nervous and glancing at the clock, claims Seat 12 is "his seat now" and grows agitated. Elliot's suspicion deepens when the manila envelope is opened — it contains not official documents, but cash bundles and a passport photo of someone else.
- **Midpoint Revelation:** Elliot realizes the bag swap happened *before* boarding — possibly at the station depot. The implications settle in: this isn't a simple mistake. Someone deliberately exchanged bags to move contraband under the cover of routine travel.

### Act 3: The Resolution
- **Climax:** The train is approaching the next major station. Elliot must decide: alert authorities and risk a delay/casualty, or confront the nervous passenger quietly. He chooses quiet confrontation, positioning himself between the passenger and the exit.
- **Falling Action:** The nervous passenger tries to flee toward the rear carriage. Elliot intercepts, not with aggression but with calm authority — "Your bag was swapped for a reason. Let's see what's inside together."
- **Ending:** The bag is surrendered. Inside: a small vial of an unknown substance, a burner phone, and a ticket stub for a different train line entirely. The police meet the train at the next stop. Elliot's vigilance prevented a potential threat, and he's commended for his attention to detail — but the image of the mismatched bag lingers, a reminder that even the most routine jobs hold hidden dangers.

### Storyboard Beats
1. **Wide shot:** Train rolling through golden countryside — establishes isolation and movement.
2. **Close-up:** Elliot's hands checking the luggage tag — texture, font mismatch.
3. **Medium shot:** Elliot opening the overhead compartment, pulling out the wrong bag.
4. **Close-up:** The manila envelope opening — cash and foreign passport photo spill out.
5. **Tight shot:** Elliot's face — realization, calculation.
6. **Medium shot:** The nervous passenger sweating, glancing at the conductor.
7. **Close-up:** Elliot's radio crackling to life — "All units, we have a situation..."
8. **Wide shot:** Train pulling into the station — law enforcement boarding.

### Themes
- **Vigilance vs. Routine:** The comfort of ritual can blind us to the anomalous.
- **Authority and Restraint:** How a person in power chooses to exercise it.
- **Passing and Identity:** The ease with which things (and people) can be mistaken for one another.

### Tone
Lean, tactile, suspenseful — like a thriller grounded in the mundane. The train's rhythm (click-clack of wheels, hiss of steam) provides a metronome for the tension, each beat bringing the conductor closer to the truth or the precipice.
