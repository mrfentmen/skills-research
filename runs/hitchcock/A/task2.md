# Lighthouse Keeper Suspense Scene Treatment

## Storyboard Summary

**Scene:** A rugged coastline, night. Storm clouds gather heavily over the sky.

**Characters:**
- **ELARA (50s)** — experienced lighthouse keeper, weary but watchful
- **The Lighthouse** — a towering structure with a failing lamp at its peak

**Beat 1 — The Watch:**
Elara stands at the lantern room window, wind whipping her shawl. She cups her hands around her eyes, scanning the dark sea. A faint outline emerges through the rain — a ship, drifting dangerously close to the jagged rocks that line the shore.

**Beat 2 — The Discovery:**
She turns from the window, moves to the central spiral staircase. Her boots echo as she descends, each step urgent. She checks the oil gauge — empty. The lamp's flame sputters, casting trembling shadows against the stone walls.

**Beat 3 — The Crisis:**
She rushes back up, striking the lamp's mechanism. It groans, the wick struggling. She pumps the lever, but the flame dims further. Through the glass, the ship lurches — a sudden gust of wind pushes it toward the invisible rocks below.

**Beat 4 — The Choice:**
Elara faces a grim reality: she can barely keep the current flame alive, let alone signal for help. She grabs a spare lantern from the shelf, fills it with the last of the oil, and races toward the emergency exit — a secondary light she can hand-carry down to the rocks herself.

**Beat 5 — The Descent:**
She bursts through the lighthouse door into the storm. Lightning flashes, illuminating the ship's outline stark against the cliffs. She sprints across the uneven ground, the lantern swinging in her grip, heart pounding in time with the crashing waves.

**Beat 6 — The Rescue:**
Reaching the rock-strewn shore, she climbs to the highest point and raises the lantern high. The beam cuts through the rain, catching the ship's captain's eye. He wheel's the vessel hard, the engines roaring as he pulls away from the dreaded rocks — just in time.

## Ending

The ship passes safely. Elara returns to the lighthouse, collapsing onto the floor, breathless. She looks up at the lamp — still flickering, still standing. A small smile. She's bought them time. The camera pulls back to show the lighthouse standing solitary against the storm, its light a fragile beacon in the dark.
