# Train Conductor Suspense Treatment

## Logline
A weary train conductor discovers a passenger's bag has been switched, triggering a tense race against time to uncover the sabotage before the next station.

## Characters
- **MARVIN HELLER** (50s), tired but diligent train conductor, 15 years on the rail line
- **ELARA** (30s), the passenger whose bag is switched, calm under pressure
- **THE MYSTERY PASSENGER** (unknown), the one who switched the bag

## Setting
- A vintage passenger train traveling through mountain passes at dusk
- The train's observation car and corridor

## Storyboard / Treatment

### Beat 1: The Routine
The train hums through the valley. MARVIN makes his rounds, checking tickets, greeting regulars. He notices ELARA in Car 3, reading a book, placing her bag overhead. She looks ordinary, exhausted but content.

### Beat 2 The Discovery
Mid-journey, during a routine baggage check, MARVIN opens ELARA's assigned compartment. The bag inside isn't hers—different color, different label. He closes it, hesitates, then opens it properly. Inside: a laptop, a passport, and a strange, bulky object wrapped in a cloth—nothing like the toiletries ELARA should have.

### Beat 3: The Confrontation
MARVIN approaches ELARA. "Excuse me, ma'am—this bag... is it yours?" ELARA's eyes flicker to the bag, then back to him. "I... I think I must have dozed off. I'm sorry, I'm not feeling well." Her composure cracks. She admits she's traveling with sensitive materials she was asked to deliver, but doesn't know who switched the bag.

### Beat 4: The Realization
MARVIN notices something on the train's announcement system—a delay at the next station, the switch could be timed. He realizes this isn't a random mix-up. The mystery passenger was aboard, seen near ELARA's compartment earlier. MARVIN checks the security footage from the corridor camera—the feed glitches at the exact moment the bag was swapped.

### Beat 5: The Stakes
The bulky object is a timed device. If it reaches the next major hub, it will detonate. ELARA offers to help MARVIN disarm it, but they need to get off the train before it triggers. The train is now two hours from the next stop. MARVIN has to choose: stop the train (risking panic and exposure) or steer it to a remote siding.

### Beat 6: The Climax
MARVIN announces a "technical delay" over the intercom, urging passengers to remain seated. He redirects the train toward an unmanned siding, cutting power to the main engine. As the train coasts to a halt, ELARA opens the bag on the observation car. Inside: a countdown timer, ticking. 3 minutes remaining.

### Beat 7: Resolution
MARVIN spring-actions the bag onto the open platform as the train rolls onto the siding. The device clicks—harmless. It was a tracking beacon, not an explosive. ELARA breathes a sigh of relief. The mystery passenger slips off at the previous station, unnoticed. MARVIN makes a note: next time, he'll double-check every bag personally.

## Notes
- Tone: *North by Northwest* meets *The Taking of Pelham One Two One*—tight, atmospheric, with a dash of old-school thriller
- Visual motifs: the train's rhythmic clicking, the glow of the corridor lights, the contrast of the bag's familiar vs. unfamiliar label
- Theme: vigilance in the mundane, the hidden in plain sight
