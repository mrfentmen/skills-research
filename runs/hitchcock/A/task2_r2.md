# Lighthouse Keeper Suspense Scene Treatment

## Storyboard Summary

**Title:** The Fading Light

**Logline:** A solitary lighthouse keeper spots a ship lost in fog, heading straight for treacherous rocks, while his lamp fails at the worst possible moment.

### Scene 1: The Lighthouse Interior
- **Time:** Midnight during a storm
- **Setting:** Small, cluttered lighthouse keeper's quarters attached to the tower
- **Action:** Old Thomas (50s, weathered face, tired eyes) is adjusting the lamp's Fresnel lens. The lamp's light sweeps across the room, casting dancing shadows.
- **Dialogue:** None needed - establishment of isolation and routine.

### Scene 2: The Observation Deck
- **Time:** Midnight
- **Setting:** Lantern room - the heart of the lighthouse
- **Action:** Thomas climbs the spiral stairs to the lantern room. He gazes out at the stormy sea. The lamp's beam rotates - *flash*. *Flash*.
- **Visual:** Lightning illuminates the dark ocean in stark flashes.

### Scene 3: The Discovery
- **Time:** Midnight + 5 minutes
- **Setting:** Lantern room, looking out to sea
- **Action:** Thomas spots a dark shape through the rain and fog. As the lamp beam sweeps across it, he makes out the silhouette of a large ship. It's listing slightly, moving erratically.
- **Critical Detail:** The ship's navigation lights are visible but confused - red and green sidelights flashing in sequence rather than the normal pattern.

### Scene 4: The Crisis
- **Time:** Midnight + 10 minutes
- **Setting:** Lantern room
- **Action:** Thomas rushes to the lamp's control mechanism. The oil pressure is dropping. The flame flickers. He realizes the fuel line has sprung a leak - the failing lamp is his first problem.
- **Urgent Action:** He has to manually pump the fuel system while keeping the lamp rotating.

### Scene 5: The Decision
- **Time:** Midnight + 15 minutes
- **Setting:** Lantern room, looking out at the ship
- **Action:** The ship is now dangerously close to the rocks - just hundreds of yards away. Thomas can see passengers on deck, confused, shining flashlights upward. He has to choose: abandon the lamp to signal with the backup handheld light, or risk staying to fix the lamp and likely fail.
- **Key Visual:** The ship's captain appears on deck, shouting toward the lighthouse.

### Scene 6: The Resolution (Cliffhanger)
- **Time:** Midnight + 20 minutes
- **Setting:** Lantern room
- **Action:** Thomas makes his choice. He decides to send a distress flare while using the handheld light to try to guide the ship away from the rocks. The main lamp sputters and dies, but the backup light cuts through the storm.
- **Final Image:** The ship veers away from the rocks, grounded safely on a sandbar. Thomas collapses against the lens, exhausted but alive.

## Theme
Isolation vs. responsibility - one man against the sea, the fragility of human ingenuity against nature's power, and the moral weight of saving strangers at personal cost.

## Visual Style
- High contrast between the warm golden lamp light and the cold blue storm
- Rain-slicked glass and metal
- The lighthouse beam as the only steady element in a chaotic world
- Slow dolly shots through the lantern room windows showing the ship's approach
