# Lighthouse Keeper — Suspense Scene Treatment

## Storyboard Summary

**Scene 1 — Interior, Lighthouse Base**
Elias (50s, weathered face, tired eyes) tends to the lamp mechanism. The great Fresnel lens sits dark except for a single pilot light. He oils a gear, sighs — another night of solitude.

**Scene 2 — Interior, Lighthouse Gallery**
Elias climbs the spiral stairs. He watches the rotating lens motor — it stutters. The bulb flickers and dies. Darkness floods the gallery.

**Scene 3 — Exterior, Lighthouse Deck**
Elias steps out onto the balcony. He cups his hands around his eyes, scanning the horizon.

**Scene 4 — Ship Deck, Night**
A massive cargo vessel plows through waves. The radar operator alerts the captain. "Contact dead ahead — rocks! Bearing 042, distance closing fast."

**Scene 5 — Exterior, Lighthouse**
Elias spots the ship. His face drains. *That's the Rockland route.* He races down the stairs.

**Scene 6 — Interior, Lighthouse Base**
Elias fumbles with a box of spare bulbs. His hands shake. The lamp remains dark.

**Scene 7 — Exterior, Cliff Top**
A secondary light — an old, forgotten marker — catches his eye through the fog. He grabs a handheld lantern and sprints across the rocks.

**Scene 8 — Ship Deck**
The ship's horn blasts. The first wave breaks over the bow. Panic sets in.

**Scene 9 — Exterior, Lighthouse Lantern Room**
Elias finally reaches the lens. He threads a new bulb, but the motor is jammed. He has to spin it by hand — the old crank method. He pumps rhythmically.

**Scene 10 — Ship Deck, Final Moment**
The lighthouse beam finally cuts through the fog, sweeping across the water. It hits the ship's hull — *just* as a breaker crashes against the rocks mere meters away. The crew sees the light, swings the ship away at the last second.

**Scene 11 — Exterior, Lighthouse**
Elias collapses against the lantern room glass, breathless. The beam sweeps across the water one more time — safe. He lets out a long, shaky breath.

## Ending Note
The lighthouse stands once more — not because the electricity held, but because a keeper refused to let the light go out.
