# Lighthouse Keeper Suspense Scene Treatment

## Information Map

# audience knows the lamp is failing and the ship is heading for the rocks the keeper does not know the ship's course the lamp bulb has cracked and the keeper believes it is just a minor flicker

## Storyboard

# three beats each tighter than the last drawn before a line was written

### Beat 1 The Flicker

- The lighthouse lamp glows steady inside the lantern room
- The keeper adjusts the Fresnel lens notices the flame dips once
- Audience sees the hairline crack in the bulb that the keeper misses
- Keeper believes the oil level is fine prepares to turn in

### Beat 2 The Discovery

- The keeper climbs the upper gallery toolbox in hand
- Removes the bulb finds the crack glints in the lamplight
- Audience sees the clock on the bulkhead 27 minutes until high tide reveals the submerged rocks
- Keeper patches the bulb with tape hopes the light will hold

### Beat 3 The Horizon

- The keeper descends and looks through the porthole
- The ship's nav lights are visible beyond the breakers much closer than expected
- Audience sees the rocks just beneath the surface the ship directly on course
- Keeper realizes the lamp will not last until the ship is near

## Ticking Clock

# the lamp oil evaporates the ship closes distance the keeper climbs each ladder tighter than the last each beat shorter than the last no relief without cost

1. Beat 1 Lamp flickers once keeper barely notices thinks oil level
2. Beat 2 Crack discovered keeper tapes bulb time index 27 minutes
3. Beat 3 Ship's lights on horizon keeper realizes imminent danger time index 8 minutes

Each beat tighter than the last no relief without cost.

## Withheld Reveal

# we never show the crash the skid marks and the silence do the work

The impact is never staged on screen. The audience hears the crash through the keeper's door the sound of breaking waves and silence afterward. What is not shown is stronger than any staging because the imagination supplies the worst moment.

## Earned Payoff

# the clock goes off here and only because we planted it in beat one

At the moment the ship strikes the lamp finally dies the cracked bulb fails exactly when the keeper looks up from the tape job. The payoff lands because the plant was honest the cracked bulb was shown in beat one the oil evaporation was implied the clock winds down in three escalating beats and goes off at the exact moment of impact.

## Named McGuffin

# the Fresnel lens drive the chase the keeper's moral responsibility is what actually matters

The lens and its bulb are the object everyone chases the keeper must keep the light burning the ship must be avoided. What actually matters is the keeper's moral responsibility the choice to fix the lamp to warn the ship to live with the consequence of the light failing.
