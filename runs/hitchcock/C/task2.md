# Lighthouse Keeper Suspense Treatment

## Information Map

Beat 1: The audience sees the ship's hull scrape the rocks while the keeper only notices the lamp flickering. The audience knows the lamp will fail; the keeper does not yet.

Beat 2: The audience watches the ship drift toward the jagged shore as the lamp goes dark. The keeper rushes up the stairs, unaware the ship is blind to the rocks.

Beat 3: The audience sees the lamp flame surge back to life just as the ship's bow grates the rocks. The keeper flips the switch, the beam cuts through darkness, and the ship veers away.

## Ticking Clock

The secret: the lamp will fail before the ship reaches safety. Pressures: Beat 1 flicker, Beat 2 darkness, Beat 3 surge. Each beat tighter than the last, no relief without cost.

## Withheld Reveal

We never show the crash. The skid marks and the silence do the work. The audience imagines the impact; the keeper feels only the light.

## McGuffin and Meaning

The lamp drives the chase: the keeper must reignite it. The crew's survival is what actually matters.

## Earned Payoff

The clock goes off here, and only because we planted it in beat one. The beam surges, the ship avoids the rocks, the keeper's duty is fulfilled.
