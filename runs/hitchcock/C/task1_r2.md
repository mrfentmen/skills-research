# TRAIN BAG SWITCH: SUSPENSE TREATMENT

## STORYBOARD

Three beats each tighter than the last drawn before a line was written

### BEAT ONE
Conductor MARIE steps into the observation car and notices the Wilson bag sits slightly ajar. The audience knows the bag was swapped at the last station while the train was in the tunnel. Marie does not know this the bags were exchanged by a station hand who noticed the Wilson family heirloom peeking from the other bag

### BEAT TWO
Marie approaches the bag and unlatches the clasp. The audience sees the heirloom wrapped in silk inside. Marie only sees a travel journal and a tin of cookies. The clock ticks the swapping happened two stops ago and the next stop is the tunnel where the real danger lies

### BEAT THREE
Marie radio the engine room and learns the train will enter the dark tunnel in ten minutes. She must decide whether to stop the train or let it run. The audience knows a weapon is in the bag the conductor does not. The clock chimes at three beats tight and no relief in sight

## INFORMATION MAP

Beat one audience knows bag swap beat one conductor does not know bag swap
Beat two audience knows heirloom in bag beat two conductor sees journal and cookies
Beat three audience knows weapon in bag beat three conductor must choose stop or go

## TICKING CLOCK

The train enters the dark tunnel in ten minutes. This is the deadline. Each beat winds the clock tighter. Beat one the bag is ajar. Beat two the heirloom is visible. Beat three the tunnel deadline arrives. No relief without cost each beat takes the conductor closer to a choice she cannot undo

## WITHHELD REVEAL

We never show the weapon the audience only hears the conductor radio for help. We never show the impact of the weapon. The imagination supplies the worse monster than any staging could. The skid marks and the silence do the work

## EARNED PAYOFF

The clock goes off here because we planted the bag swap in beat one. Marie stops the train before the tunnel. The payoff lands because the plant was honest
