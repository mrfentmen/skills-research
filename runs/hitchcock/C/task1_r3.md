# THE BAG SWITCH

## STORYBOARD

### BEAT ONE
Conductor ARTHUR notices the newcomer's bag looks different the color does not match the car decor. The audience knows the bag was swapped at the last station while the train paused. Arthur does not know this the bag was exchanged by a nervous traveler.

### BEAT TWO
Arthur mentions the bag to the passenger who becomes defensive. The audience sees a glimpse of silver in the switched bag. Arthur only sees a travel umbrella and a paperback. The clock ticks the train is making up time soon approaching the next stop.

### BEAT THREE
Arthur discovers the clasp is broken the bag does not quite close. The audience knows the real owner is searching for the bag at the next station. Arthur must decide whether to act. The clock winds tighter.

## TICKING CLOCK
The train approaches the mountain pass in twenty minutes. This is the deadline. Each beat winds the clock tighter. Beat one the bag looks different. Beat two the defensive passenger. Beat three the pass arrival no relief without cost each beat takes the conductor closer to a choice she cannot undo

## WITHHELD REVEAL
We never show the swapper's face the audience only hears. We never show the bag's contents fully. The imagination supplies the worse monster than any staging could. The silence and the shadow do the work

## EARNED PAYOFF
The clock goes off here because we planted the bag swap in beat one. Arthur stops the train at the station. The payoff lands because the plant was honest
