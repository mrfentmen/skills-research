## Treatment

**Title:** THE LIGHT THAT FAILED

**Logline:** A solitary lighthouse keeper discovers his lamp has failed just as a ship carrying mysterious passengers steers blindly toward the rocks in the gathering storm.

**Act I: The Keeper**
Elias has tended this lighthouse for twenty years. The rhythm of oil, wick, and glass is his life. But age is slowing him, and the modern automation system he was promised never arrived. Tonight, the lamp flickers—just a minor issue, or so he tells himself.

**Act II: The Discovery**
Through his telescope, Elias spots a ship off course. Its running lights are visible, but it's not following the channel. It's heading straight for the infamous rocks that have claimed dozens of vessels. As he watches, the ship's course becomes clearer—it's not a mistake. Someone wants it wrecked.

**Act III: The Choice**
The lamp fails completely. Elias has fuel, but no wick adjustment can save the bulb. He could abandon the light to confront the figures at his door—wreckers, perhaps, or something more personal. Or he could light the backup lantern and risk the climb in the gathering dark.

The storm breaks. Lightning reveals the ship's silhouette against the moon. Elias makes his choice: he dons his coat and heads for the door, not to fight, but to light the ancient emergency light—the one that burns whale oil and has been unused for years.

**Final Image:**
The emergency light flickers to life, casting a desperate beam across the water just as the ship grinds against the rocks. Elias watches from the window, the light catching tears on his face as the hull breaks apart. The survivors wash ashore near the lighthouse, and Elias moves to help, the light now burning steady behind him.

## Notes

- Themes: isolation, responsibility, the collision of old and new
- Visual motif: the contrast between the failing electric lamp and the steady emergency flame
- Suspense built through: limited resources, ticking clock, unseen threat at the door
