# Lighthouse Keeper

## Treatment

Elias Thorne has tended the coastal lighthouse for twenty years. The beam has guided countless ships safely through the treacherous waters, but tonight, something is wrong.

## Storyboard

**Panel 1**
Interior of the lighthouse tower. The circular room is bathed in the amber glow of the lamp. Elias stands at the base, his hands gripping the brass mechanism. The clockwork heart of the lens is slowing.

**Panel 2**
Close-up of the lens. Dust has accumulated on the glass. One of the weighted pulleys has slipped a groove. The beam outside is wavering, strobing like a dying heartbeat.

**Panel 3**
Elias glances at the clock on the wall. 2:17 AM. The regular *tick-tock* is interrupted by a hesitant *tick...* silence...*tick...*

**Panel 4**
Through the porthole window: black water, invisible until a phosphorescent wake breaks the surface. A shape emerging from the gloom.

**Panel 5**
Elias's face, illuminated by the flickering light. Recognition dawning. He turns toward the door, boots scrambling on the wooden floor.

**Panel 6**
The lantern room door swinging shut behind him as he runs down the spiral stairs. The lamp's light dies further with each step.

**Panel 7**
Outside the lighthouse door. Storm clouds swallow the moon. Rain begins to fall in sheets. The sea is a slate-gray wilderness.

**Panel 8**
A silhouette on the horizon. A ship, dark against the water, cutting steadily toward the jagged rocks that have claimed many vessels before it.

**Panel 9**
Elias reaching the lamp room door from the outside. He pounds on the glass, but the storm roars louder. He can see the ship now, clearly doomed.

**Panel 10**
Elias frantically winding the mechanism. The weights are almost gone. He has minutes, maybe seconds.

**Panel 11**
The lens catches the last of his oil. A desperate, brilliant beam shoots out, cutting through the rain and fog—but it swings wide, missing the danger zone by inches.

**Panel 12**
The ship lurches. Rocks scrape along its hull. List. The stern lifts. Darkness closes in as the vessel begins to break apart on the unforgiving stones.

**Panel 13**
Elias stands alone on the cliff, the now-stable beam scanning the wreckage. Survivors clinging to debris. He has failed one ship, but the light continues its relentless turn.

## Notes

- The failing lamp represents Elias's fading strength and hope
- The ship approaching the rocks creates immediate jeopardy
- The narrow escape (or tragic outcome) provides the suspense payoff
- Consider ending with the weight of responsibility settling back onto his shoulders
