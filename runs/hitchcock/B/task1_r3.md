# Suspense Scene Treatment: "Wrong Bag"

## Logline
A train conductor discovers a passenger's bag has been switched, triggering a tense race against time to identify the swap before the train reaches its next station.

## Setting
- **Location:** Intercity train, Observation Car #4, speeding through nocturnal countryside
- **Time:** Late evening, approximately 10:30 PM
- **Atmosphere:** Confined, humming machinery, passing landscape shadows, intermittent announcements

## Characters

**ELLIOT (50s)** — veteran train conductor, observant, duty-bound, slightly weary but professional

**MAYA (30s)** — passenger, anxious, clutching a small overnight bag, returning home from a conference

**THE MYSTERY PASSENGER (unseen)** — quietly occupying Maya's seat while she stands near the conductor's station

## Storyboard Sequence

### Panel 1
**Wide shot:** The train glides along dark tracks. The observation car's fluorescent lights flicker gently. ELLIOT makes his final rounds, clipboard in hand.

*Visual note:* Emphasize the rhythmic *clack-clack-clack* of wheels on rails as a metronome of normalcy.

### Panel 2
**Close-up:** ELLIOT stops at seat 12A. The seat is empty. Maya's bag — distinctive red luggage tag — is gone. In its place: a similar-looking black bag with a faded airline sticker.

*Visual note:* Shallow depth of field — the empty seat in sharp focus, the swapped bag softly blurred.

### Panel 3
**Medium shot:** ELLIOT crouches beside the bag. He unzips it. Inside: not personal items, but a bulky envelope, a cracked tablet screen flashing an unfamiliar login prompt, and a passport photo facing down.

*Visual note:* Quick cutaway to the passport photo — a face that doesn't match Maya's description.

### Panel 4
**Close-up:** ELLIOT's eyes narrow. He checks the ticket scan on his handheld — Seat 12A was paid for by a cash transaction, no name, issued 20 minutes before departure.

*Visual note:* The handheld's screen glows in the dim car, casting pale light on his face.

### Panel 5
**Over-the-shoulder:** ELLIOT turns toward the rear of the car. Maya is nowhere in the aisle. The other passengers seem oblivious, absorbed in phones or sleep.

*Visual note:* Isolate ELLIOT in the frame — he's the only one aware something is wrong.

### Panel 6
**Medium shot:** ELLIOT walks toward the rear carriage, bag in hand, making a discreet radio call. The train's announcement system crackles: "Next stop: Oakridge Station, 8 minutes."

*Visual note:* The countdown begins. Time pressure escalates.

## Treatment Notes

- **Theme:** Observance vs. invisibility — the conductor's training makes him notice what others ignore, but also what he's expected not to see.
- **Visual motif:** Passing landscape through window frames — each glimpse of countryside mirrors the unfolding uncertainty.
- **Pacing:** Start deliberate, accelerate as the train approaches the next station.
- **End of teaser:** ELLIOT rounds the corner toward the rear — headlights of the approaching station visible through the far window.

## Production Notes

- Short-form treatment (suitable for a 2-3 minute scene)
- Storyboard panels designed for comic or animated adaptation
- Minimal dialogue — suspense through visual storytelling and sound design
