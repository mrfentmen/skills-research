# Lighthouse Keeper Suspense Treatment

## Scene Setup

**Location:** remote coastal lighthouse, night during a violent storm
**Time:** 3 AM - the darkest hour, lamp mechanism groaning

## Characters

**ELAINE (50s)** - experienced lighthouse keeper, weathered by years of service, instincts sharp despite exhaustion

**THE SHIP** - vague silhouette through the driving rain, navigating blind

## The Crisis

The lighthouse lamp - the keeper's eye, her beacon of hope - is failing. The Fresnel lens is cracked, the oil reserve is low, the mechanism jamming in the salt-spray. Each rotation of the light that should cut through the darkness instead sputters and dies.

Elaine watches the chart table. A blip on the radar, barely distinguishable from the storm noise. A ship. Too close. Too fast.

## Storyboard Panels

### PANEL 1
Ext. Lighthouse - Night
The camera pushes through driving rain and howling wind. The lighthouse beam sweeps across the turbulent ocean in jerky, failing rotations. Oil drips from the mechanism onto the glass floor.

**SFX:** *Thwump. Thwump. Thwump.*

### PANEL 2
Int. Lighthouse - Gallery
Elaine's hands move with automatic precision, winding the mechanism. Her face illuminated by the strobing light - one moment bright, the next pitch black. She glances at the chronometer: 2 minutes since last failure.

### PANEL 3
Ext. Ocean - Night
Through the rain-spattered window of the lighthouse, a shape emerges from the darkness. The ship's mast - jagged against the storm. Running lights flickering, erratic. It's not following the lane of light. It's heading straight for the rocks to the north.

### PANEL 4
Int. Lighthouse - Keepers' Room
Elaine drops the wrench. She rushes to the window, pressing her forehead against the cold glass. The ship's outline is clearer now - listing, taking on water, engines dying. The crew doesn't know where they are. They can't see the light.

### PANEL 5
Int. Lighthouse - Gallery
Elaine throws herself back at the mechanism. Both hands on the winch. She winds frantically, faster and faster, ignoring the grinding metal. The light struggles to catch - a single revolution, then darkness again.

**ELAINE**
*(muttering)*
Come on... you piece of junk... don't you dare die on me...

### PANEL 6
Ext. Rocks - Night
The ship strikes. Not a catastrophic crash, but a grinding, inexorable collision against the hidden reef. Splintering wood, shouting voices, the desperate scrambling of survivors. The light finally catches - sweeping across the chaos - but too late for some, too early for others.

### PANEL 7
Int. Lighthouse - Gallery
The light stabilizes, cutting through the storm. The radar blip is gone, replaced by the quiet of the aftermath. Elaine sinks to the floor, exhausted. The ship is gone. The rocks are safe. But at what cost?

## Treatment Notes

- **Visual motif:** The failing light as a metaphor for fading hope
- **Pacing:** Start slow, build through the panels, climax at panel 6, resolve in 7
- **Sound design:** The heartbeat of the mechanism matching Elaine's own panic
- **Thematic question:** When your life's work is failing, do you fight or accept?
